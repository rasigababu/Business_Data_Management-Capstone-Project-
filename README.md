# 📊 Business Data Management – Capstone Project

## 📖 Overview
This repository contains my academic capstone project focused on analyzing the performance of a retail store using business and data management principles.
---

## 🎯 Project Objective
The main objectives of this project were:

- To analyze sales and procurement patterns
- To identify reasons for low profitability
- To study inventory and credit management
- To provide data-driven business recommendations
- To improve overall operational efficiency
---

---

## 🧠 Methodology
The project followed a structured data analysis approach:

- Data collected from manual store records
- Organized using spreadsheets
- Cleaned and structured for analysis
- Analyzed using statistical and visualization techniques
- Findings summarized in formal reports
---

## 📊 Key Focus Areas
- Revenue and expense analysis
- Inventory management
- Credit vs cash transaction analysis
- Product demand patterns
- Profit and loss evaluation
- Business performance monitoring
---

## 🗣️ Stakeholder Communication
Special emphasis was placed on presenting insights in a simple and understandable format using charts, summaries, and business-friendly language.

This helped non-technical stakeholders clearly understand findings and take informed business decisions.

---
## 📈 Project Impact
After implementing the recommendations provided in the final report, the retailer observed:
- ✅ Approximately **20% increase in transaction volume**
- ✅ Improved inventory turnover
- ✅ Better management of credit sales
- ✅ More structured business record-keeping

This demonstrates the practical value of data-driven decision-making in improving real-world business performance.
